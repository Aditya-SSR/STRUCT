## 1. The one-line idea

**A garment is a structure. Every line you can see is a line that is carrying something.**

Tagline (fixed by the founder): **FORM FOLLOWS FUNCTION.**
Short form: **Wear the structure.**
Studio rule (the method in one sentence): **If it doesn't carry, cut it.**

The tagline's three words — FORM / FOLLOWS / FUNCTION — are also the three principle words on the home page (carried over from the original build), set in Monument Extended.

STRUCT = *structure*. Also the programmer's keyword: a thing that holds other things together. Both meanings are intended.

## 2. The story (canonical — all copy derives from this)

### Tokyo, 2019 — The Week We Quit
Four people who had never worked together resigned from four different design jobs in the same seven days. An architect. A pattern cutter. A textile engineer. A type designer. Each had the same reason, said four different ways:

*They were being asked to add something to a thing that was already finished.*

A print. A logo. A "detail". Decoration laid over structure so it would look like it had an idea. They had stopped believing that was design. They thought it was packaging for a missing idea.

They met on a Sunday in Kiba, Tokyo, on the top floor of a closed rope factory. The floor still had rope-weight marks burned into the concrete, and the light came from the north only. They stayed. The factory is the studio today.

### The rule on the wall (2020)
First thing pinned up, written by Ren in marker on a roll of pattern paper:

> **IF IT DOESN'T CARRY, CUT IT.**

That became the whole method. They call it **KŌZŌ** (構造, "structure"). It has three steps:

1. **Load** — where does the body push on the cloth, and the cloth on the body? Map it before touching a pencil.
2. **Line** — draw only where load travels. Every seam, panel and tape is a load line.
3. **Resolve** — remove everything else. What is left is the garment. We do not "design" a piece. We *resolve* it.

### The Field Studies (2021 – 2025)
For four years they made single garments the correct way. Eight of them: a grid-line shell, a base layer, a rigid hood, an asymmetric tee, a masked base top, a cargo shell, a modular vest, a floor-length coat. All finished. All right. All wrong.

Each one was only half a structure. A shell needs what is under it. A hood needs the coat it was cut against. A garment alone is a **part**, not a thing. They retired all eight and kept the notes (the Field Studies appear on the site as the archive, marked RETIRED).

### The Suit Problem (2025)
The realisation, said by Mio during a fitting, recorded in the studio log:

> "We keep selling the beam and calling it the building."

The unit is not the garment. The unit is the **body, dressed**. Coat, tunic, trouser and belt are cut as one structure, so their load lines continue across each other: the white tape that starts at the shoulder of the coat is still running when it reaches the ankle of the trouser. Take one piece away and the line breaks.

So they stopped selling parts. **SUIT-01** is sold whole, or not at all.

### The Nodes (2026)
Seven more studios opened in one year — not stores first, **nodes**: small, identical rooms with a cutting table, a mirror and a north window. Tokyo is the origin; the rest are mirrored copies of it. A node exists to *resolve* locally: pattern and fit are re-checked on local bodies before a drop ships to that region.

### Drops
STRUCT does not run seasons. It runs **drops**: a fixed number of units, released once, never restocked. When a drop is gone the structure is closed. The number is part of the product.

**DROP-01** — released 14 Nov 2026, 12:00 JST. **400 units** total. One object: SUIT-01.

## 6. Visual identity — "Structure, drawn"

The site should feel like a **technical drawing that is slowly being resolved into a garment**: hairline grids, registration marks, dimension callouts, dot-matrix numerals — in a white studio that matches the photography.

### 6.1 Colour
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | `#0A0A0A` | Black. Text, NOIR sections, menu |
| `paper` | `#F3F4F2` | Studio white (matches the photo backgrounds). Default page |
| `bone` | `#E6E8E6` | Alternate light surface |
| `ash` | `#B9BEBF` | Hairlines on paper, secondary text, floor-grey from the photos |
| `smoke` | `#6C7274` | Tertiary text |
| `signal` | `#FF5A00` | **The only colour.** Carried over from the original STRUCT. One signal per viewport, max: a datum, a live dot, a hover. Never a background wash |

The page alternates **NOIR** (ink) and **BLANC** (paper) sections — the two colourways of the suit, used as the site's rhythm.

### 6.2 Type — three fonts, three jobs
| Font | Job | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Monument Extended** (`--font-monument`) | Display. Wordmark, headlines, menu items, product names | UPPERCASE only (no bold exists). Big. Tracking −0.03 to −0.05em above 4rem. Tight leading 0.9–1.0 |
| **Bitcount Prop Single** (`--font-dots`) | Data. Counters, numerals, drop numbers, clock, stock, "01 / 08" | The dot-matrix voice. Numbers and short labels only. Never body copy. Has no arrow glyphs — use Monument/Space Mono for → |
| **Space Mono** (`--font-mono`) | Body. Paragraphs, captions, specs, nav, buttons, labels | 12–16 px. Sentence case for paragraphs, UPPERCASE + 0.08em tracking for labels |

(Space Mono stands in for the "Nothing"-style mono in the original brief; if the real Nothing typeface files turn up, swap the `--font-mono` variable only.)

### 6.3 Graphic language
- **Load lines** — 1–2 px lines (white on ink, ink on paper) that run through layouts and **draw themselves** on scroll/enter. The garment's seam tape, abstracted. The signature motif.
- **Datums** — small `+` registration crosses at grid intersections and image corners. Corner brackets `⌜ ⌟` around key objects (carried over from the original build).
- **Callouts** — dimension lines and figure labels: `[ FIG. 03 — LOAD LINE ]`, `↔ 1024`, `35.6762° N 139.6503° E`.
- **Dots** — dot-matrix numerals and a dot-grid texture. The globe is dotted for the same reason.
- **Zero radius.** No rounded corners, no shadows, no gradients except photographic ones. Borders are 1 px hairlines.
- **Photography** — raw rectangles, full-bleed or hairline-framed. Studio white and cool grey only. Never filtered, never tinted.
- **Status language** (kept from the original build): `SYS.STATUS: ACTIVE // NODE 01`, `OBJ.01`. Used for labels, sparingly.

### 6.4 Layout
12-column grid, 24 px gutters (16 px mobile), page margin 32 px (16 px mobile). Sections are full-viewport "plates" with a hairline frame and numbered `§01`, `§02` markers. Large type may break the grid; images may not.

### 6.5 Motion — "Resolve"
Things don't fade or bounce. They **resolve**: construction lines draw first, then content is revealed from behind masks and settles.
- Eases: `expo.out` for reveals, `power4.inOut` for covers and the menu, `power2.out` for hovers. **No elastic/back/bounce.**
- Durations: reveals 0.9–1.3 s, hovers 0.3–0.5 s, covers 0.9–1.1 s. Stagger 0.03–0.06.
- Text: line/word masks (`overflow:hidden` + `yPercent`) — never opacity-only.
- Scroll: Lenis smooth scroll, GSAP ScrollTrigger, `scrub` for the signature sequences only.
- Cursor: a small datum `+` that grows to a labelled ring on interactive elements (`VIEW`, `DRAG`, `ADD`).
- Everything has a `prefers-reduced-motion` path (instant states, no scrub, no preloader choreography).

### 6.6 Voice
Calm, exact, a little dry. Engineers describing something beautiful without saying so. Short declaratives. No exclamation marks. No "elevate / unlock / curated / journey".
- Yes: "If it doesn't carry, cut it." / "Sold whole." / "300 units. Once."
- No: "Discover our exclusive new collection!"
